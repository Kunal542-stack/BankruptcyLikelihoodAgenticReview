# CREATD, INC. (CIK 1357671): 10-Q for the period ending 2023-09-30, filed 2023-11-15, model score 0.523

## Answer

The score comes from three numbers: an annualized net loss of $31.0M on only $2.9M of assets (5th peer percentile), liabilities of 8.16x assets (peer median 0.85), and a current ratio of 0.02 (peer median 0.35). The filings confirm the distress. Many notes are in default, and about $4.75M of debentures all fall due on February 28, 2024. If anything, the score understates the risk.

## Verdict

- **Verdict:** real risk
- **My own risk:** high (about 25%)
- **Agree with the model:** yes

## Main reasons

1. **Huge losses on a tiny asset base** (model input). Net income is -$31.0M, or -1073% of assets, at the 5th peer percentile. Operating income is at the 19th. No quote (model input).

2. **Liabilities far exceed assets** (model input). Liabilities are $23.6M against $2.9M of assets (8.16x vs a peer median of 0.85), at the 82nd peer percentile. No quote (model input).

3. **Almost no liquidity** (model input, plus the filings). Current assets cover about 2% of current liabilities (ratio 0.02 vs a peer median of 0.35). The filing says: "The uninsured cash balance as of September 30, 2023, was $0." (10-Q, 2023-11-15, Summary of significant accounting policies). *My inference:* there are roughly 1 to 6 months of cash left. Q3's low burn was propped up by $5.9M of unpaid payables and accruals.

4. **Going-concern doubt and widespread defaults** (from the filings). The company warns: "These factors raise substantial doubt about the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern for a period of one year from the issuance of these financial statements." (10-Q, 2023-11-15, Note 3 – Going Concern). It also reports: "As of September 30, 2023, the Company is in default on $2,265,447 in principal on senior secured notes from The July 2022 Convertible Note Offering." (10-Q, 2023-11-15, Part II, Item 3).

5. **Too much debt comes due at once** (from the filings). About $4.75M ($2,485,000 + $2,267,140) is due February 28, 2024. About $92,600 of new monthly payments also start in late 2023. Holders agreed to "extend the maturity dates for the Debentures, which have a cumulative remaining balance of $2,485,000, to February 28, 2024" (8-K, 2023-11-01, Item 1.01).

6. **Secured lien, and new money is drying up** (from the filings). Secured holders have a first-priority lien on all assets. The company says: "If we do not have sufficient funds and are otherwise unable to arrange financing, our assets may be foreclosed upon" (10-K, 2023-04-19, Item 1A Risk Factors). The conversion price was cut from $0.20, as holders agreed to "reduce the conversion price of the Debentures down to $0.025" (8-K, 2023-11-01, Item 1.01).

## Evidence against

- **Lenders extend rather than enforce:** "agreed to extend the maturity dates for the Debentures, which have a cumulative remaining balance of $2,267,140 including interest and fees, to February 28, 2024." (8-K, 2023-11-01, Item 1.01 (Extension Agreements))
- **Much of the loss is non-cash** (from a $23.3M nine-month loss): "Stock-based compensation for stock options has been recorded in the consolidated statements of operations and totaled $8,283,267 for the nine months ended September 30, 2023." (10-Q, 2023-11-15, Statement of Cash Flows; stock options note)
- **Burn is falling** (-$4.5M vs -$18.5M): "Going forward, the Company expects the loss from operations to decrease significantly as it focuses on organic revenue growth and continues to find efficiencies to reduce operating expenses." (10-Q, 2023-11-15, MD&A – Results of Operations)
- **Liabilities are settled with stock:** "The Company and Executive agreed to settle the outstanding liability of $405,208 in exchange for $75,000 in cash payment and 5,753,472 shares of the Company’s common stock." (8-K, 2023-11-01, Item 1.01 (Settlement Agreement))

## How it developed

- **2022-04-30:** PPP loan goes into default.

- **2023-04-19:** 10-K filed with an auditor going-concern paragraph.
- **2023-09-30:** $2,265,447 of senior secured notes in default.
- **2023-10-16:** CFO resigns. The CEO also becomes CFO.
- **2023-11-01:** Debentures extended to 2024-02-28 and conversion price cut to $0.025.
- **2023-11-15:** NT 10-Q, then this 10-Q with going-concern doubt.

## Dates to watch

- **2023-11-30 and 2023-12-11:** first monthly payments on the July 2023 notes
- **2023-12-15:** deferred note payments due
- **2023-12-31:** Second September 2022 Note matures
- **2024-02-28:** about $4.75M of debentures due
- **Fiscal 2023 10-K:** another NT 10-K? The new auditor's going-concern view?

**What would change my mind:**
- **Lower risk:** a raise in the millions that covers the February maturities, or holders converting most of the $4.75M or extending well past 2024.
- **Higher risk:** a foreclosure, an enforced judgment, or a missed monthly payment.
